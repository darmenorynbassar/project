# Literature

Reading list for the honours project *Development of an AI-Based Student Academic Support System*.
The project's core is early identification of at-risk students on OULAD; a support layer turns predictions into a human-reviewed advisor letter and meeting proposal.

**Status key**
- ✅ checked: details confirmed against the source (publisher page, dataset page or full text)
- ⚠️ verify: details still to be checked on Google Scholar / publisher before final submission

Citations are in APA 7th edition (Google Scholar → "Cite" → APA).

---

## 1. Dataset (primary)

- ✅ Kuzilek, J., Hlosta, M., & Zdrahal, Z. (2017). Open University Learning Analytics dataset. *Scientific Data, 4*, 170171. https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.171
  Download: https://figshare.com/articles/OULAD_Open_University_Learning_Analytics_Dataset/5081998 (CC BY 4.0). 22 module presentations, 32,593 students. **Primary dataset**, approved by supervisor.
- ⚠️ Choi, Y., Lee, Y., Shin, D., Cho, J., Park, S., Lee, S., Baek, J., Bae, C., Kim, B., & Heo, J. (2020). EdNet: A large-scale hierarchical dataset in education. In *Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED 2020)*, LNCS 12164 (pp. 69–73). Springer.
  Mentioned by supervisor as an alternative. CC BY-NC 4.0. **Not used** unless the scope changes; do not cite in the proposal unless used.

## 2. OULAD-based and at-risk prediction (core of the project)

- ✅ Jin, L., Wang, Y., Song, H., & So, H.-J. (2024). Predictive modelling with the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD): A systematic literature review. In A. M. Olney et al. (Eds.), *AIED 2024 Workshops* (CCIS 2150, pp. 477–484). Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64315-6_46
  17 OULAD studies (2017–2024); themes: performance, at-risk, engagement; calls for more interpretability. **Key paper.**
- ✅ Albreiki, B., Zaki, N., & Alashwal, H. (2021). A systematic literature review of student' performance prediction using machine learning techniques. *Education Sciences, 11*(9), 552. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci11090552
- ✅ Sekeroglu, B., Abiyev, R., Ilhan, A., Arslan, M., & Idoko, J. B. (2021). Systematic literature review on machine learning and student performance prediction: Critical gaps and possible remedies. *Applied Sciences, 11*(22), 10907. https://doi.org/10.3390/app112210907
- ✅ Alalawi, K., Athauda, R., & Chiong, R. (2023). Contextualizing the current state of research on the use of machine learning for student performance prediction: A systematic literature review. *Engineering Reports, 5*(12), e12699. https://doi.org/10.1002/eng2.12699
  162 studies, Jan 2010–Oct 2022; classification dominates; very few studies test remedial actions.
- ✅ Hassan, S.-U., Waheed, H., Aljohani, N. R., Ali, M., Ventura, S., & Herrera, F. (2019). Virtual learning environment to predict withdrawal by leveraging deep learning. *International Journal of Intelligent Systems, 34*(8), 1935–1952. https://doi.org/10.1002/int.22129
  LSTM on OULAD clickstream: accuracy 97.25%, precision 92.79%, recall 85.92% (abstract).
- ✅ Waheed, H., Hassan, S.-U., Aljohani, N. R., Hardman, J., Alelyani, S., & Nawaz, R. (2020). Predicting academic performance of students from VLE big data using deep learning models. *Computers in Human Behavior, 104*, 106189. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2019.106189
  Deep ANN 84–93% accuracy; LR 79.82–85.60%, SVM 79.95–89.14% (accepted manuscript; check published numbers).
- ⚠️ Hlosta, M., Zdrahal, Z., & Zendulka, J. (2017). Ouroboros: Early identification of at-risk students without models based on legacy data. In *Proceedings of LAK '17* (pp. 6–15). ACM.

## 5. Chatbots, RAG and generative AI (support layer only)

- ✅ Özer, M. F., Akpınar, M. T., & Ceyhan, S. (2026). Digital student services: Intelligent chatbot systems supported by artificial intelligence and RAG model. *Journal of Entrepreneurship and Innovation Management, 15*(1), 272–287. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/jeim/article/1940101
  RAG chatbot in a student-affairs portal; designed from 20 interviews, evaluated by 40 students (ratings).
- ✅ Modran, H. A., Bogdan, I. C., Ursuțiu, D., Samoilă, C., & Modran, P. L. (2024). LLM intelligent agent tutoring in higher education courses using a RAG approach. *Preprints*. Not peer-reviewed. https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202407.0519.v1
  LlamaIndex + OpenAI API RAG tutor; also cited as an ICL 2024 conference paper.
- ✅ Miladi, F., Psyché, V., & Lemire, D. (2024). Leveraging GPT-4 for accuracy in education: A comparative study on retrieval-augmented generation in MOOCs. In *AIED 2024 Workshops* (CCIS 2150, pp. 427–434). Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64315-6_40
- ✅ Badger, M., López, N., & Nissen, C. F. R. (2026). The impact of GenAI chatbots on student learning in higher education: A literature review. *International Journal of Technology in Education, 9*(1), 43–69. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijte.5111
  49 empirical studies, 2022–2024; benefits (personalised help, feedback) and risks (over-reliance, integrity).
- ✅ An, Q., Koh, J. H. L., & Liu, Q. (2026). Generative artificial intelligence in higher education: A systematic review of student use and learning outcomes. *Australasian Journal of Educational Technology, 42*(3), 62–82. https://doi.org/10.14742/ajet.10561
  39 studies; seven types of student use; actual outcomes mostly positive, perceived outcomes vary.
